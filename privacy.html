Privacy Policy

Effective: December 2026

Who we are

This website provides professional information about Rebecca Winter, M.A., LPC, LPCC. It is informational only. Rebecca is not accepting new private clients at this time, and visiting or contacting through this site does not create a therapist-client relationship.

Information we collect

This website does not use contact forms, cookies, or analytics tracking. If you email, we receive only what you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and message.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. These providers may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, as part of delivering the site.

Please don't send health information by email

Email is not a secure way to share personal health information. Please keep messages brief and general.

How we use your information

  • To respond to professional inquiries

We do not sell or share your personal information.

Your choices

To ask about or request deletion of information you've shared, email rebecca@rebeccawintercounseling.com.

Accessibility

We aim to make this website accessible to everyone. If you have trouble using any part of it, please email us and we'll be glad to help.

Crisis support

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
