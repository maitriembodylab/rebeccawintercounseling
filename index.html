Your body often knows what you need before your mind does.

I offer warm, trauma-informed therapy for adults and couples. My work blends talk therapy with body-centered approaches, so insight doesn't just stay in the mind. It settles into the nervous system, where lasting change happens.

My approach

I believe the deepest healing happens when we treat the mind and body as one integrated system. Therapy with me begins with safety and connection. Together, we get curious about how your nervous system responds to stress, and we build steadiness from there.

I'm collaborative, warm, and grounded. You set the pace, and you decide what to share and when.

My training spans somatic psychology, transpersonal counseling, and evidence-based trauma treatment. I draw on whichever tools best fit your goals, rather than following a single script.

The aim isn't only to talk about change, but to feel it: calmer reactions, kinder self-talk, and more secure connection with the people you love.

Methods I use

EMDR

A structured, research-supported approach for processing trauma and distressing memories, paced with care and plenty of resourcing.

Somatic and Polyvagal-informed therapy

Body awareness, breath, and grounding practices that help your nervous system find safety and regulation.

IFS-informed and Gestalt parts work

Getting to know the different parts of you, including the inner critic, with curiosity and compassion.

Gottman Method couples therapy

Assessment-guided work to strengthen friendship, manage conflict, and rebuild trust (Levels 1 and 2 training).

Mindfulness-based approaches

Present-moment skills drawn from mindfulness, MBSR, and mindfulness-based CBT and DBT.

Transpersonal and existential

Space for meaning, values, and the spiritual dimensions of life, whatever your beliefs or background.

Who I work with

Adults (roughly ages 23 to 65), individuals and couples, navigating:

  • Anxiety and stress
  • Depression and low self-worth
  • Trauma and PTSD
  • Relationship challenges, betrayal, and trust repair
  • Grief and life transitions, including midlife and empty nesting

I offer affirming care to clients of all identities, including LGBTQ+ clients and couples of all orientations.

You may be a good fit if…

  • You're curious and growth-oriented, and want to understand yourself more deeply.
  • You're open to exploring the mind-body connection, not only talking things through.
  • You feel stuck in patterns that insight alone hasn't shifted.
  • You want a warm, collaborative therapist who lets you set the pace.
  • You and your partner are ready to work on connection with practical tools.

About Rebecca

My path began more than two decades ago in bodywork, where I learned how stress and emotion are held in the body. That led me to a B.A. in Somatic Psychology from Prescott College and an M.A. in Transpersonal Counseling Psychology from Naropa University.

Over eleven years of clinical work, I've supported clients in rape crisis and domestic violence settings, community mental health, and private practice. That breadth shapes how I work: grounded, ethical, and deeply trauma-informed.
